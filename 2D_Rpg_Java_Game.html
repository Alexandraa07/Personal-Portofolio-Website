THE LEGEND

A long time ago, a magical forest was protected by three nature spirits: the Spirit of Light, the Spirit of Water, and the Spirit of Earth. Together, these spirits maintained the balance of nature and protected the forest from any danger, while the Ancient Tree, the heart of the forest, powered all the creatures.

One night, an unknown force appeared in the forest and captured two of the spirits. Dark magic spread everywhere, and the Ancient Tree was drained of all its powers. The only spirit that managed to escape is the Spirit of Light, Luma — the character controlled by the player. Will Luma be able to save her family and restore balance to the magical forest?
